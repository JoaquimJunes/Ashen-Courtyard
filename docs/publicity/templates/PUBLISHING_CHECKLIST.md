# Publicity publishing checklist

Copy this checklist into the episode working document. A failed truth, rights or
privacy item blocks publication; an optimization item may be deferred deliberately.

## Truth and scope

- [ ] The post states whether the subject is implemented, implemented for review,
      a visual prototype, an approved concept or planned work.
- [ ] Historical footage is labeled as an earlier version.
- [ ] Placeholder stations and deferred mechanics are not presented as playable.
- [ ] Numbers, controls and implementation claims match the linked project docs.
- [ ] The opening, title and thumbnail make the same promise.
- [ ] The selected community question does not bypass an existing design gate.

## Footage and edit

- [ ] The approved result appears before extended background or channel branding.
- [ ] Normal-speed gameplay appears before slow motion or frame-by-frame analysis.
- [ ] The failure shown teaches a specific lesson and has a visible payoff.
- [ ] The production character/game path is distinguished from staged pose previews.
- [ ] 16:9 footage is sharp and stable at the intended viewing size.
- [ ] Vertical footage or crop keeps action and UI legible inside safe zones.
- [ ] Obsolete behavior, debug text and cursor movement are removed unless explained.
- [ ] The final edit was watched once on desktop and once on a phone.

## Voice, audio and accessibility

- [ ] English voice-over is intelligible without music.
- [ ] Music and sound effects do not hide game audio needed for the explanation.
- [ ] Captions match the final narration and identify meaningful non-speech audio.
- [ ] Burned-in short-form captions do not cover the character, HUD or controls.
- [ ] On-screen text is concise, high-contrast and readable on a phone.
- [ ] Flashing, rapid cuts or intense motion receive an appropriate warning when needed.

## Rights, credits and privacy

- [ ] [CREDITS.md](../../../CREDITS.md) was reviewed against every visible asset.
- [ ] The creator is introduced and credited as **Neth** only.
- [ ] No legal name, personal email, location or private account is present.
- [ ] Required attribution, including PSX Dungeon when visible, is in the description.
- [ ] Quaternius/UAL-derived clips and local derivatives are described accurately.
- [ ] The unresolved PSX Sword / Espada PS1 asset does not appear anywhere.
- [ ] Music, sound, fonts, images and graphics have recorded usage permission.
- [ ] A paid partnership, gift or affiliate relationship is disclosed when applicable.
- [ ] Local usernames/paths, notifications, tokens and private files are absent.
- [ ] Image, audio, video and document metadata contains no personal identifiers.
- [ ] No contributor identity or private discussion is shared without permission.

## Package and upload

- [ ] Title is accurate, specific and understandable without project history.
- [ ] Thumbnail remains legible at small size and does not misrepresent the result.
- [ ] Description includes the truth boundary, community question, credits and
      relevant development sources.
- [ ] YouTube captions, chapters, playlist, end screen and audience setting are checked.
- [ ] Reel/Short uses a clean 9:16 master and platform-specific additions were made last.
- [ ] Upload preview was watched before publication.
- [ ] Final files and project/source links are archived with the episode record.

## After publication

- [ ] Correct any material error in the description or a pinned comment.
- [ ] Answer substantive questions and useful criticism before generic reactions.
- [ ] Record the 7-day review without changing several packaging variables at once.
- [ ] Record the 28-day review and update the topic backlog with the lesson.
