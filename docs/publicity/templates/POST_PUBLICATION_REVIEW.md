## Decision

Choose one action in each category. Avoid changing every variable at once.

- **Keep:** one topic, structure, hook or production choice that worked.
- **Change:** one clearly defined variable for the next comparable post.
- **Stop:** one step that cost time without improving clarity or community value.
- **Explore:** one audience question that belongs in the publicity backlog.
- **Do not treat as approval:** feedback that conflicts with or precedes the
  project's design/review process.
