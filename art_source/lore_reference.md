# World lore — Soul, crystals and Skulk

Source: lore supplied directly by Neth for future concept art. Preserve these statements as user-authored reference. The questions below remain unresolved; no answer or gameplay mechanic is inferred.

## Established reference

- **Soul:** the energy that fuels every kind of life.
- **Death releases Soul:** when a living being dies, its Soul leaves the body. It lingers briefly nearby, then disperses if not gathered. Its ultimate fate remains mysterious; the exact duration is undefined. Magic circles may gather this nearby released Soul. This does not authorize automatically draining nearby living beings.
- **Crystals:** colonies of molecule-sized beings that feed on souls to reproduce. Their crystal appearance comes from a hardened protective shell. The material grows and spreads by consuming magic.
- **Skulk:** a plague or curse that possesses flesh, mutates its host and takes control of the body. The host's soul remains trapped within the cursed body.
- **Strong-Soul resistance:** a strong Soul can resist Skulk and reclaim movement, but cannot automatically cure the infection. The host can remain visibly mutated while conscious and in control. This allows affected enemies to hesitate, resist their own attacks, or briefly communicate. Neth explicitly approved adding this to Skulk lore; specific enemy behaviors are not implemented by this approval.

## Open questions from Neth

- What happens when the player becomes contaminated?
- What are the limits and duration of strong-Soul resistance? Resistance is established; a cure or permanent mastery is not.
- Do crystals consume replenishable Soul energy, the individual Soul itself, or both under different conditions?
- How, if at all, is Skulk related to the protagonist's stone arm?

Neth explicitly deferred the crystal-feeding and stone-arm-connection questions. Keep them unresolved and focus current development on completing the magic-system concept. Do not reopen them or infer answers as a dependency for rune/circle work.

## Boundaries for future concepts

The relationship among Skulk, the protagonist's stone transformation, his hand crystal and the rune inscriptions has not been established. Do not turn a design suggestion into confirmed lore or replace approved character features. The existing six maximum-life stone stages remain their own approved visual progression; they are not contamination stages.

It is not yet settled whether crystals consume replenishable Soul energy, irreversibly consume an individual soul, or have different feeding behavior under different conditions. This affects any future interpretation of the hand vessel and its contents.

The visual appearance of Skulk and the internal organization of crystal colonies have not been approved. No new images, contamination system, powers, penalties, cure or integration are authorized by this lore reference alone.
