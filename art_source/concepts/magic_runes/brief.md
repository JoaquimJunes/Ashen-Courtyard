## Approved decisions

- Retro Lowpoly Dark Fantasy; mana-gauge artwork is the visual authority.
- A — Ancient Chisel is the approved surface treatment. Keep visible polygon geometry: broad facets, angular carved recesses, bevels and chips. Follow [style notes](style_notes.md).
- Twelve core runes, preserving recognizable existing shapes and standardizing inconsistent strokes.
- A shared ancient concept language with recognizable variations between cultures.
- Players recognize meanings in spells, objects and environmental clues. Casting uses predefined spells.
- Focus on soul, flesh, stone, binding and transformation.
- Core meanings are reliable; complex ancient inscriptions retain mystery.
- Persistent inscriptions on bodies, objects and ritual sites, plus brief projected circles during casting.
- Explicit meaning-to-shape approval: Neth answered, “Yes this meaning-to-shape mapping works, lets start developing the visual rune dictionary”.

## Deliverable and acceptance

One static twelve-entry dictionary with clean glyphs, unchanged source crops, names, definitions and source locations. Editable vector construction is appropriate because this extends an existing symbol system and its drawing coordinates, rather than exploring three alternative art directions. The meanings are approved; dictionary v01 visual execution still awaits review.

Check all twelve entries and their source mapping, compare the two crown traces to the painted reference, ensure text does not overlap, and flag similar silhouettes without silently redesigning them. Dark charcoal presentation and restrained violet strokes borrow the gauge palette. These reference colors do not establish every culture's material or magic color.
