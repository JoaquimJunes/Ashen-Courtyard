# Approved rune style — Ancient Chisel

Neth approved [A — Ancient Chisel v01](style_exploration/direction-a-v01.png) and explicitly requested low-poly visuals with visible polygon geometry.

## Visual requirements

- Preserve the twelve approved rune identities, orientations, main strokes, branches and meanings.
- Use deep angular incisions, substantial carved edges and restrained irregularity in stroke width.
- Show visible polygon geometry in the stone surfaces, bevels, chipped edges and rune recesses. Use broad planar changes in light and shape; a fine polygon texture over a smooth surface is not sufficient.
- Retain charcoal stone and violet illumination within the grooves. Keep cavity walls and facets readable around the light.
- Preserve the selected image as the approved reference. Do not silently substitute B/C, smooth the stone, or rework its light intensity based on earlier agent preferences.
- Treat small surface noise as secondary to readable facets and silhouettes. Avoid making photoreal microtexture the dominant visual feature.

## Application and boundaries

Game-wide reusable direction: Retro Lowpoly Dark Fantasy with intentional, visible faceted geometry. Rune-specific direction: the twelve approved glyphs, chiseled groove treatment and the selected charcoal/violet presentation.

Future projected circles should express an angular visual language consistent with this reference, but circle geometry, activation and behavior are still subject to design review. Do not infer those mechanics from the artwork. HUD, shaders, game assets and runtime behavior are not changed by concept approval.

The new approval does not authorize additional generated images. Obtain permission before another generation round; also obtain permission before internet reference searches or interactive previews. Preserve earlier versions and ask review questions in plain text.
