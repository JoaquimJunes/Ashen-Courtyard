## A — Hovering

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Generate ONE landscape paired concept-art sheet in Retro Lowpoly Dark Fantasy, with TWO equally prominent panels. Landscape 1536x1024 or larger. This is a Soul-gathering magic circle shown as a physical ritual inscription and a floating magical projection of the SAME design.
REFERENCE 1 is the approved twelve-rune dictionary: copy glyph SHAPES from its large clean symbols. REFERENCE 2 is the approved Ancient Chisel material: deep angular carved charcoal stone, polygon facets and violet light. DO NOT render either reference as a dictionary grid or HUD.
SHARED CIRCLE COMPOSITION, EXACT in both panels:
Exactly TWO concentric TWELVE-SIDED polygon rings; visibly straight segments and distinct corners, not smooth circles. Seven runes inside EACH circle, each appearing ONCE. No extra runes to fill the ring circumference.
Center: Vessel to the left, Anchor to the right, separated and never merged.
Middle/operation ring: Soul at 12 o'clock, Draw at 4 o'clock, Path at 8 o'clock.
Outer/containment ring: Boundary at 12 o'clock, Bind at 6 o'clock.
These clock positions are in the circle's own plane, then projected naturally with camera perspective. Runes lie at geometric positions along the ring lines with clear space around their strokes. Keep all glyphs oriented toward the top of the circle in its own plane for readability, not randomly rotated. Thin angular connecting paths link the operation nodes toward the central pair, while ring outlines stay visibly continuous around glyph nodes. Connections must not look like additional glyph strokes.
Copy the reference silhouettes faithfully: Soul = tall top-bar stem with right hook and crossing diagonal; Anchor = long stem crossed by two ascending diagonals with small bent top; Vessel = upright stem with enclosed slanted flag; Draw = leftward-pointing triangle and inward short stroke; Path = vertical crossing stem with lower bent angular structure; Boundary = stem with upper triangular branch and lower diagonal; Bind = upright R-like branched rune.
Do NOT use the other five dictionary glyphs. Do NOT add a ring full of random occult letters. Do NOT mirror the glyphs.
LEFT PANEL labeled "CARVED RITUAL": one large readable near-top-down, slightly oblique view of a charcoal stone ritual slab. The circle and seven runes are deeply chiseled into it, with broad angled groove walls, faceted chipped edges, subtle violet light within the incisions. The slab remains intact through the essential inscriptions; chips are at the edge. Enough surrounding stone environment is visible to show environmental marks. Keep this composition comparable across all variants.
RIGHT PANEL labeled "PROJECTED CASTING": the identical seven-rune two-ring design appears as a single freestanding thin plane of violet magical light in the environment. Negative spaces are transparent; the environment is visible THROUGH the circle. No solid disc, no magical wall, no rune panel pasted onto a slab. Polygonal light segments, sharp vertices and controlled bloom preserve readability. The projection must visibly float unsupported.
ENVIRONMENTAL INSCRIPTIONS REQUIRED IN BOTH PANELS: include a few secondary approved Soul, Bind or Path marks on nearby walls or floor fragments, outside the main circle. Some are dark deep stone carvings; some are DRAWN IN DRIED BLOOD, matte muted dark burgundy/red-brown pigment with restrained uneven hand-painted edges. Make at least one blood-drawn rune readily noticeable, but subordinate to the main circle. These are background inscriptions, NOT additional nodes or extra glyphs within the seven-rune circle. No blood pools, bodies or people. Blood is a visual inscription medium here, not a new power-source explanation.
ENVIRONMENT / STYLE: empty ancient ritual chamber, charcoal broken stone floor, angular architecture, broad visible LOW-POLY POLYGON GEOMETRY on slabs, chips, pillars and planes. Flat/faceted shading, simplified painted texture. NOT photoreal surfaces and NOT smooth stone with a triangle texture laid on top. No dense microtexture. Cool neutral ambient fill makes geometry visible, restrained violet magic, muted dried-blood marks as the only red accent. No characters, hands, weapons, skulls, creatures, HUD, crystals, labels for individual runes, decorative extra sigils or scene clutter. Stay readable rather than pitch black.
Keep both panels generously scaled with a quiet narrow dividing gutter and a restrained title above. Only text: exact title and the two panel labels.

POSITIONING VARIATION:
RIGHT PANEL: the circle hovers diagonally above a fractured stone floor, tilted approximately 35 degrees so its face is readable and its unsupported height is obvious. Camera sees it from a slightly elevated three-quarter angle. Violet reflected light on the floor, but no second ground circle. Dried-blood rune on a nearby floor fragment, carved marks on the rear wall. LEFT PANEL stays near-top-down carved ritual study.
Exact sheet title: "A — HOVERING". Produce exactly ONE paired concept sheet.
```
