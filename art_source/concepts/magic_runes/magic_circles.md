# Magic circles — approved concept rules

Status: foundational structure, activation, sources, damage distinctions and the first paired composition brief approved through Neth's choices. Direction A is approved as the visual reference; exact grammar and gameplay values remain undeveloped. No implementation is authorized by these lore/design choices alone.

## Structure

- Concentric rings with visible faceted polygon geometry.
- Runes distributed in geometric patterns along the ring lines.
- The center identifies the subject or anchor.
- Inner rings describe operations; the outer ring defines the effect's boundary.
- Use the twelve approved runes and their meanings, with the [Ancient Chisel treatment](style_notes.md).
- Persistent inscriptions and briefly projected casting circles share the rune language. Their precise construction, reading order and connection grammar still need a visual/design review.

## Activation

A circle needs a Soul supply and an activation signal. Casting uses conscious intent; persistent inscriptions may use predefined activation conditions. Being powered alone does not automatically activate an otherwise dormant circle. An unpowered circle cannot produce its effect.

The required instruction must be valid. The exact validity rules, trigger representations and power requirements are not yet defined. No new trigger rune is implied by this rule.

## Soul sources

Approved designated sources: the caster, a connected vessel or a prepared reservoir. Nearby living beings are not automatically drained by proximity.

Neth added an exception for recently dead beings: when a living being dies, its Soul leaves the body; circles can gather that nearby released Soul. Neth selected brief presence: Soul lingers nearby, then disperses if not gathered, with its ultimate fate remaining mysterious. This establishes an available source, not an automatic collection mechanic for every circle. Capture range, exact linger duration, capacity, collection timing, ownership and collection behavior remain undefined.

See the [lore reference](../../lore_reference.md). Do not use this rule to answer the deferred crystal-feeding question or assign a Skulk origin to the stone arm.

## Damage

- Broken action runes stop their affected operations.
- Broken containment can release energy.
- Consequences depend on what part is damaged; an arbitrary scratch does not establish universal failure or an explosion.

Exact dependency handling, discharge form, severity and gameplay consequences remain to be designed. An inactive or empty inscription is not assumed to contain energy it does not have.

## First worked composition — approved brief and direction A

Use two concentric twelve-sided polygon rings for the Soul-gathering example. Place Vessel and Anchor at the center; Soul at 12 o'clock, Draw at 4 and Path at 8 on the operation ring; Boundary at 12 and Bind at 6 on the outer containment ring. Each of the seven glyphs appears once in each main circle. Clock positions refer to the circle plane before perspective, with glyphs upright toward its top. Connect the composition using angular routes without inventing additional symbols.

Include both carved and dried-blood runes on surrounding stone. The blood marks establish a visual inscription medium only, not blood as fuel or a new casting cost.
