## A1 — PRECISE RITUAL

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Create ONE landscape Retro Lowpoly Dark Fantasy concept sheet, two equally sized side-by-side panels, 1536x1024 or larger. This is a new visual experiment derived from reference 1's APPROVED paired stone-carved ritual and projected violet magic circle. Reference 2 is the authoritative rune dictionary: use only the large rune silhouettes, not its source thumbnails.
WHITE DIMENSION: replace the dark chamber with an expansive warm-white/off-white seamless spatial canvas like a character concept sheet. White background AND ground, soft grey cast shadows grounding a few angular charcoal stone fragments. Lots of clean white negative space. Small isolated stone structures may establish perspective; never a dark room or a rectangular dark backdrop. No people. Reference 1's BLOOD MARKS ARE EXPLICITLY DEFERRED: absolutely NO blood, red marks, painted inscriptions or banners. A few dark stone-carved dictionary runes on peripheral stones are allowed.
Two labeled panels: left CARVED RITUAL, right PROJECTED CASTING. Left shows a complete charcoal ritual slab from slightly oblique near overhead; right shows a transparent unsupported violet light projection of the SAME design above white ground. No solid disc, no portal, no opaque violet interior. Matching topology, glyph count and symbols across both panels.
GEOMETRY: exactly TWO concentric regular TWELVE-SIDED polygon rings, twelve straight segments and twelve distinct corners each. Ring lines remain continuous. Do not accidentally make hexagons, octagons or decagons. Vertex indices in the instructions below start at 0 at the top, clockwise 1..11; these indices are invisible construction guidance, DO NOT print them. Runes are positioned at selected vertices, NOT scattered in the center. Unoccupied corners stay empty. Exactly SEVEN rune glyphs per circle, each once: Soul, Draw, Path, Vessel, Anchor on the inner ring; Boundary at outer vertex0 and Bind at outer vertex6. Preserve reference2's exact glyph silhouettes and orientation; keep them upright in circle coordinates, do not mirror or rotate them independently. Draw is the LEFT-pointing triangle, not Stone's right-pointing triangle. Bind has an upright stem. Thin connecting lines join occupied vertices to create the requested internal drawing; connector lines are clearly distinct from the thicker rune strokes. No new symbols, extra alphabets or additional circles. Keep glyphs readable at junctions.
STYLE: visible low-poly polygon geometry, broad angular stone planes, faceted groove walls, charcoal matte material, violet carved illumination and projected light with clear angular edges. Soft neutral illumination; violet still legible on white with purple cores and controlled halos. No photoreal microtexture, smooth round bevels or excessive bloom. Geometry visibly built from flat planes, not a triangle texture on smooth objects. Keep scale, framing, white background and presentation comparable to companion sheets.
Only print the supplied sheet title and CARVED RITUAL / PROJECTED CASTING. No numbered vertices, explanations, legends or other text.

EXPERIMENT:
Narrow crisp engravings, sharp regular corners, evenly controlled stroke weight, thin precise connecting routes. INNER GLYPH POSITIONS: Soul0, Draw4, Path8, Vessel2, Anchor10. The internal drawing is one clear large TRIANGLE connecting vertices0→4→8→0. Vessel2 and Anchor10 stay on the inner ring, outside this triangle's sides; no extra central strokes. RIGHT projection is upright, standing vertically in open white space, viewed near-frontal with a slight three-quarter perspective. A small broken pillar beside it establishes height. This is NOT low hovering or horizontal.
Exact title: A1 — PRECISE RITUAL. Generate exactly ONE paired sheet.
```
