# Awakening of the Fallen Warrior — generation prompts

Method: built-in image generation. Three separate generation calls, one per direction.
Each initial v01 sheet then received one targeted built-in image edit to correct
the mirrored hand inset. Current review deliverables are v02; v01 is retained.
These are exploratory concepts, not approved final artwork or game assets.

## Reference image roles

- `<PROJECT_ROOT>/art_source/references/MainKnightReference.png`
- `<PROJECT_ROOT>/art_source/references/retro_style_reference.png`
- `<PROJECT_ROOT>/art_source/ui/soulbound_hud/concepts/evolution-partial-closeup.png`

The character sheet guides build; the retro knight guides faceted painted surfaces;
the main mana gauge guides the ritual stone, crystal, liquid, runes and sockets.
External Confessor / Dark Crusader references supply only the already-agreed
costume context; no external image was supplied to these generation calls.

## A — Close-fitting stone

Use case: stylized-concept.
Create ONE original game character concept sheet, landscape 3:2 composition, Retro Lowpoly Dark Fantasy. This is a readable rough design study, not a cinematic tomb scene or a photorealistic painting.
Reference roles: MainKnightReference.png guides broad muscular warrior build and grounded adult male proportions only; face/hair may be reinterpreted. retro_style_reference.png guides angular/faceted form and hand-painted materials, but reduce coarse pixel noise. evolution-partial-closeup.png is the PRIMARY ritual material/object reference: weathered charcoal stone, angular rune shapes, purple liquid, translucent crystal and exactly five circular recesses. Do not paste the HUD onto the body: omit hearts, horizontal health panel, UI, floating labels and large crown spikes.
Layout: ONE full-body FRONT three-quarter standing character occupies the left 60%, barefoot on a pale neutral warm-gray background; an enlarged dorsal LEFT hand study occupies the right 40%. Subtle floor shadow, readable soft neutral illumination, clear restrained colors and comparable detail across body and close-up. Show the same design in both views. No extra characters, inset faces or unrelated props. Only a small unobtrusive direction title at top. The character stands with his anatomical LEFT arm relaxed slightly away from his body, turned so the back of that hand is visible. Anatomical LEFT is on viewer's RIGHT in the main view. Avoid mirrored sides. Empty hands.
Character: broad-built mature human male warrior awakening from an ancient tomb; short dark hair, worn and quietly confused expression, no helmet. Minimal opaque worn burial cloth around waist/upper legs, comfortably covering groin and buttocks; chest and left arm exposed. No full armor, robes covering the arm, weapons, jewelry or invented extra magic objects. Readable faceted low-poly shapes and restrained painted texture surfaces, broad color shapes rather than noisy triangulated mosaic.
FIXED ANATOMY: charcoal stone is rooted in approximately half of his LEFT pectoral, crosses left shoulder, and continues over the ENTIRE LEFT arm through elbow, forearm, wrist and hand. Irregular embedded flesh/stone transition; right chest and right arm remain normal skin. This is integrated stone skin, not a strapped gauntlet, sleeve, removable armor or separate prosthesis. Preserve human joints, five fingers and believable movement. No blood or exposed wounds.
RUNE PATH: visibly engraved magical runes form a continuous SPIRAL wrapping down from shoulder around upper arm and forearm into the back of the hand. Restrained dim violet in grooves; inscriptions legible through their carving, not overwhelming glow.
HAND OBJECT: centered on the back of the LEFT hand, a clear translucent semi-spherical/hemispherical dome crystal seated into stone, SMALL ENOUGH TO FIT the hand. It protrudes modestly above the hand. Within it a clearly visible horizontal liquid surface divides an EMPTY TRANSLUCENT upper HALF from a dark-purple LIQUID lower HALF. Exactly half full. Faint submerged eye-like shapes sit entirely below the liquid surface, subtle and dim, not bright white eyes or a face painted over the glass. The dome is faceted lightly to fit low-poly design while retaining its hemispherical silhouette. One engraved rune ring circumscribes the dome in the surrounding stone. Immediately BELOW the ring, TOWARD THE WRIST, exactly FIVE SMALL EMPTY CIRCULAR SOCKETS in ONE shallow arc. Five separate dark recessed holes, no gemstones, liquid, glyphs, extra rows or additional circular sockets. Keep sockets off finger joints. In hand close-up, fingers point UP and wrist is DOWN, so the five socket arc is below the orb. Hand close-up must include wrist and all five natural fingers with correct left-hand anatomy.
Mood reference: cold heavy darkness, a ceiling fragment falls, one shaft of light reveals an open tomb, the knight wakes and pulls himself upright with his left hand, then discovers his transformed arm in confusion. Convey ancient burial wear and restrained unease, while KEEPING this concept sheet evenly lit and uncluttered.

Direction A: Close-fitting stone. Broad quiet charcoal-stone surfaces follow the pectoral, deltoid and arm muscle volumes. Minimal raised edges; narrow weathered fissures. The spiral is an intentional carved path across these broad surfaces. Burial cloth is a simple desaturated ash-beige wrapped waistcloth with an uneven knee-length hem and small folded waist tuck. Emphasize calm readable body contours. Top title: A — CLOSE-FITTING STONE.
