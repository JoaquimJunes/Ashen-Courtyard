# Life progression — six forms of the stone arm

Design approved for exploration on 29 September 2026. Artwork awaits Neth's
visual review. This is a static concept-art pack, not an implemented game system.

## Selected direction and behavior

Use **B — Fractured Stone**, with the corrected dorsal left-hand anatomy from
[B v02](../direction-b-v02.png). Permanent **maximum-life upgrades** strengthen
the stone. Damage and healing do not change its permanent stage. Match the mana
gauge's six forms (one starting state plus five transitions), while keeping life
and mana upgrades independent. Exact life thresholds are deferred to gameplay
design; no numeric thresholds are implied by the stage numbers.

## Six visual stages

| Stage | Form | Intended change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | Fractured | B baseline: separate angular fragments, deep fissures, chipped edges. |
| 1 | Binding | Small fissures begin closing; visible stone bridges join neighboring fragments. |
| 2 | Reinforced | Smaller pieces join into larger sections; loose-looking edges become firmly connected. |
| 3 | Consolidated | Broad continuous surfaces dominate; few deep fractures remain. |
| 4 | Fortified | Dense sturdy stone, shallow residual seams, firmly fused edges. |
| 5 | Unbroken | Intact matte stone with broad faceted planes, restrained weathering and readable articulation. |

Strength comes from material continuity, not greater bulk, wider coverage or
polished surfaces. Preserve the initial coverage boundary and exposed fingertips.
Necessary joint seams and intentional rune grooves are not damage cracks.

## Invariants

- Same identity, proportions, pose, opaque burial clothing, lighting, camera and palette.
- Same left chest/shoulder/arm/hand coverage, approximate volume and silhouette.
- Same shoulder-to-hand engraved rune spiral, count and restrained violet accents.
- Same hand crystal: half-full purple liquid, faint submerged eyes, surrounding
  rune ring and exactly five empty sockets in a wristward arc.
- Correct dorsal left-hand close-up: thumb on viewer right, fingers up, wrist down.

Each stage is generated as an edit of the same B v02 source to limit accumulated
drift. These are still generated concept sheets, not registered production textures
or proven deformation models. Visual inspection is recorded in the review notes;
it does not substitute for Neth's approval or later in-game tests.

## Deliverables and review

Open [the six-stage comparison](index.html) or use the individual PNG links there.
The contact view shows all six; select a stage for a full-size sheet and use the
arrow controls to inspect adjacent stages. The page is local, static and offline.

Review adjacent stages for visibly decreasing fracture density, then compare 0
and 5 for stable identity and shape. Inspect the hand at full size for five
sockets, a half-full dome and correct handedness. Confirm the last form still
reads as stone and allows believable human articulation.

Current review status: awaiting Neth's feedback. Refinement, transition animation,
exact maximum-life thresholds and game integration are separate next steps.
