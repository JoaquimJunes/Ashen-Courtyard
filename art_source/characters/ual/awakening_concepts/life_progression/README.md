## Approved visual direction

Use these selected images as the authority for fracture density and exposed
skin at each stage. Do not substitute the smoother later correction attempts.
Stone becomes more connected and flesh progressively petrifies; the final form
has stone fingertips and retains the selected angular surface divisions.
The six static sheets define visual intent, not exact numerical coverage masks.

Subject-specific choices: broad warrior proportions, worn burial cloth, a fixed
left-chest transformation region, dark charcoal stone, varied carved violet
runes along the shoulder-to-hand spiral, correct dorsal left-hand orientation,
a half-filled purple crystal with faint eye-like forms, and five empty sockets.
Stone continuity and skin coverage express permanent maximum-life upgrades.
Damage and healing do not reverse stages; mana progression remains independent.

Reusable style notes: deliberate low-poly planes, readable silhouettes, restrained
texture detail and violet accents, matte stone, and worn cloth within **Retro
Lowpoly Dark Fantasy**. These are reusable references; exact character anatomy,
runes and ritual-object geometry belong to this subject rather than every asset.
Neutral study lighting supports comparison. In-game lighting remains unvalidated.
