# Six life forms — visual inspection

Agent inspection, 29 September 2026. All six v01 sheets were inspected at their
native 1536 × 1024 size. Final visual approval remains Neth's decision.

## Adjacent-stage comparison

| Comparison | Observed distinction |
| --- | --- |
| 0 → 1 | Deep dark breaks between small hand fragments close into connecting surfaces; the stone remains heavily fragmented. |
| 1 → 2 | Medium sections become distinct around fingers, dorsal hand and chest, replacing many small fragment borders. |
| 2 → 3 | Hand and forearm surfaces join into larger areas with fewer separating breaks. |
| 3 → 4 | Residual seams become narrower and shallower; shoulder and forearm surfaces read as denser joined plates. |
| 4 → 5 | Most remaining stone seams disappear into broad uninterrupted planes; dark joint articulation and intentional rune grooves remain. |

The change is clearest in the enlarged hand. The 3/4 distinction is subtler than
the endpoints and should be reviewed at full size, especially before reducing
these designs to gameplay scale. No numerical crack-density measurement is claimed.

## Preserved design checks

- All six keep the same recognizable face/build, pose, burial cloth, neutral
  background, lighting arrangement and full-body-plus-hand composition.
- The transformed arm is on the character's anatomical left. Enlarged left-hand
  views have five fingers, thumb on viewer right and preserved exposed fingertips.
- Five empty circular sockets remain below the crystal in the wristward arc.
- The crystal retains a visible approximately half-full horizontal purple liquid
  level, faint submerged eyes and surrounding rune ring.
- The violet spiral remains visible as intentional engraving, separate from
  the fracture progression. No new mana stages or filled reserves appear.
- Stone stays charcoal, matte and faceted at the final stage, with broadly stable
  size and coverage. Finger joints retain articulation separations.

## Limits and pending review

Generated sheets preserve design continuity, not exact pixel registration. Small
surface and outline differences remain, and stage 0 reinterprets some fracture
details from the selected B source. These are concept references rather than
production textures, model topology, animation frames or proven deformation.
The apparent ability to articulate is not a rigging test. Gameplay-distance
readability and precise maximum-life thresholds remain future validation.
