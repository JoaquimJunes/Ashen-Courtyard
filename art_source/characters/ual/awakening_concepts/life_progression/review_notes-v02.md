# Six life forms — revision 02 visual inspection

Agent inspection, 29 September 2026. All six selected 1536 × 1024 sheets and the
six-column browser comparison were inspected. Final concept approval remains
Neth's decision; no gameplay implementation status changed.

## Adjacent-stage comparison

| Comparison | Observed distinction |
| --- | --- |
| 0 → 1 | Shorter flesh caps, narrower finger-side/wrist skin strips, and broader connected stone sections. The thumb shaft becomes mostly stone. |
| 1 → 2 | Finger-side, thumb-base and wrist flesh disappears; short fingertip caps and a small main-figure elbow patch remain. Medium stone sections replace smaller fragments. |
| 2 → 3 | Fingertip caps shrink further, the elbow patch nearly disappears, and hand/forearm planes broaden with fewer deep divisions. |
| 3 → 4 | Only tiny tip ends remain flesh; most damage seams fuse into continuous stone, with shallow residual seams. |
| 4 → 5 | The final flesh traces and residual damage seams disappear. Fingertips and nails are stone; anatomical articulation and rune grooves remain. |

The changes are clearest in the enlarged hands. Adjacent stages are visually
ordered; no quantitative crack-density or skin-area measurement is claimed.

## Preserved design checks

- Dark charcoal retains readable facets in all six stages.
- Forked, hooked, crossing and diamond-based glyphs replace the repeated baseline
  symbol. The main glyph landmarks and spiral route remain consistent.
- Same recognizable identity, body proportions, pose, clothing and lighting.
- Correct left side on the figure and dorsal left-hand inset with thumb viewer right.
- Exactly five empty sockets remain below each enlarged crystal.
- The liquid remains approximately half full and distinctly purple. Submerged
  eye-like forms remain very faint and ambiguous at comparison size; inspect
  the full sheet before approving their readability.
- Stage 5 visibly petrifies the entire shown arm/hand and fingertips. Palm
  petrification is specified by the brief but the palm is not directly shown
  in this dorsal view.
- Stone remains matte and faceted, with intentional finger/wrist/elbow seams.

## Corrections and limitations

Initial later-stage drafts exposed too much flesh and retained excessive damage
fractures. The selected revisions address those issues. Stage 1 was regenerated
directly from the revised Stage 0 after its first correction still exposed too
much flesh. All attempts remain preserved and mapped in the revision manifest.

Small glyph, contour and chest-edge variations remain between generated sheets;
they are not pixel-registered production templates. The same general chest
footprint is retained, but a fixed production mask should come from the approved
baseline. No additional torso spread is part of the design. These images do not
prove rigging, articulation, hidden palm coverage or gameplay-distance readability.

## Delivery checks

The offline comparison loads all 12 image views across six equal-width aligned
columns. Full-sheet modal navigation, bounds, keyboard arrows and Escape passed.
At a 390-pixel viewport the comparison scrolls horizontally without widening the
document. Images copied into the project match their generated sources and all
have the expected dimensions. Earlier v01 sheets and review remain preserved.
