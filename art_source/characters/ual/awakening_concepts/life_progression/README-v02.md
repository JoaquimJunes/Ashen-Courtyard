# Life progression — six forms of the stone arm

Revision 02: darker charcoal, varied rune symbols, progressive skin petrification
and six aligned comparison columns, approved for exploration on 29 September 2026.
Artwork awaits Neth's visual review. This is a static concept-art pack, not an
implemented game system. Earlier sheets and their comparison remain archived.

## Selected direction and behavior

Use **B — Fractured Stone**, with the corrected dorsal left-hand anatomy from
[B v02](../direction-b-v02.png). Permanent **maximum-life upgrades** strengthen
the stone. Damage and healing do not change its permanent stage. Match the mana
gauge's six forms (one starting state plus five transitions), while keeping life
and mana upgrades independent. Exact life thresholds are deferred to gameplay
design; no numeric thresholds are implied by the stage numbers.

## Six visual stages

| Stage | Form | Intended change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | Fractured | Selected baseline with darker charcoal and varied runes; exposed skin remains. |
| 1 | Binding | Small fractures join; thin stone growth advances along exposed skin boundaries. |
| 2 | Reinforced | Larger sections fuse; arm flesh patches shrink and stone advances along fingers. |
| 3 | Consolidated | Broad surfaces dominate; only small flesh patches remain at joints and fingertips. |
| 4 | Fortified | Dense stone with shallow seams; tiny flesh traces remain at finger ends. |
| 5 | Unbroken | Entire left arm and hand, including palm and fingertips, are intact stone; joints remain articulated. |

Strength comes from material continuity and actual petrification of the remaining
left arm/hand flesh, not greater bulk, applied armor or polished surfaces. The
existing chest boundary stays fixed. Exposed fingertips remain only in early
stages and progressively turn to stone. This supersedes the v01 fixed-coverage
and permanently exposed-fingertip rules. Necessary joint seams and intentional
rune grooves are not damage cracks.

## Invariants

- Same identity, proportions, pose, opaque burial clothing, lighting, camera and palette.
- Same chest boundary, approximate arm/hand volume and overall silhouette;
  exposed left arm/hand skin decreases at every stage.
- Slightly darker charcoal with readable facets and fracture edges; unchanged
  normal skin, cloth, lighting, crystal colors and background.
- Varied angular rune family (forks, hooks, intersecting strokes and diamond forms)
  preserving the spiral's route, spacing and restrained violet glow. New rune
  shapes stay consistent across all six v02 stages.
- Same hand crystal: half-full purple liquid, faint submerged eyes, surrounding
  rune ring and exactly five empty sockets in a wristward arc.
- Correct dorsal left-hand close-up: thumb on viewer right, fingers up, wrist down.

## Deliverables and review

Open [the six-stage comparison](index.html) or use the individual PNG links there.
The comparison has six aligned columns, with equally scaled characters above
matching enlarged hands. Narrow screens scroll horizontally instead of stacking
the stages. The page displays cropped regions of the original PNGs without
altering their files. Select an image for its full sheet, then use arrow controls
to inspect adjacent stages. The page is local, static and offline.

Review adjacent stages for visibly decreasing fracture density AND exposed flesh,
then compare 0 and 5 for stable identity and shape. Stage 5 must have no flesh
remaining on the left arm or hand. Inspect the hand at full size for five
sockets, a half-full dome and correct handedness. Confirm the last form still
reads as stone and allows believable human articulation.
