# Life progression — exact generation prompts

Method: built-in image generation, six separate edit calls against the same
corrected [B v02 reference](../direction-b-v02.png). The approved stage count
and permanent maximum-life behavior are visual design decisions, not implemented
gameplay. Relative fracture fractions below are generation targets, not measured
physical or image statistics.

## 0 — Fractured

Use case: precise-object-edit / stylized-concept.
Edit the supplied B — Fractured Stone v02 character sheet to make ONE stage in a six-image progression of the SAME character. Output a single landscape 1536x1024 sheet matching the reference. This is a controlled concept art variation, NOT a new character design.
KEEP EXACTLY: character face/hair/body proportions and expression; pose and hand position; bare feet; right side normal skin; opaque worn dark waist cloth; main character placement on left; enlarged dorsal LEFT hand on right; camera, crop, relative sizes, warm pale gray background, neutral lighting, color palette and Retro Lowpoly Dark Fantasy painted/faceted style. Do not change normal skin shading or cloth. Copy all unedited areas from the reference as closely as possible.
ONLY CHANGE: the internal fracture condition of the integrated charcoal stone on the anatomical LEFT pectoral/shoulder/arm/hand, and the sheet title. Preserve the SAME outer stone coverage boundary on chest and arm, the SAME volume and silhouette, and existing exposed skin fingertips. This is permanent maximum-life progression: material fuses, not growing armor or spreading stone. Stone strength is conveyed by fewer/decreasing cracks and increasingly joined, larger surfaces. Edit both the main figure AND the enlarged hand consistently.
INVARIANT RITUAL DESIGN: preserve exactly the original engraved violet spiral route from shoulder to hand, existing glyph shapes, glyph count, brightness and positions. Rune grooves remain engraved even when damage cracks disappear; do not add runes, ornaments or magical effects. Preserve the hand crystal dimensions and position, transparent hemispherical faceted dome, dark-purple liquid filling precisely its lower half with a horizontal surface, faint submerged eye-like shapes, rune ring and exactly FIVE EMPTY CIRCULAR SOCKETS in ONE shallow arc TOWARD THE WRIST below the crystal. Do not fill, move, add or remove sockets. No mana upgrades; no extra glow; no changing fill level.
LEFT HAND ANATOMY: in the right-side enlarged BACK-OF-LEFT-HAND inset, fingertips point UP, wrist DOWN, thumb on VIEWER RIGHT, pinky on VIEWER LEFT, exactly five fingers. Keep it identical to this correctly oriented reference. The figure's transformed left arm remains on viewer RIGHT.
Six stages are 0 Fractured (baseline), 1 Binding (small bridges), 2 Reinforced (medium joined sections), 3 Consolidated (large continuous planes with few fissures), 4 Fortified (only sparse shallow seams), 5 Unbroken (no damage cracks). Preserve articulation at human joints throughout. This request generates ONLY the stage named below, NOT a multi-stage grid.

REQUESTED STAGE 0: FRACTURED
BASELINE. Retain ALL stone fragments, gaps, deep fissures and chipped edges of the reference exactly. Only update the title. No healing of stone at this initial stage.
Replace the original top title with exactly "0 — FRACTURED". No other captions, numeric health values or text.
