# Awakening narrative reference

Neth's source script, preserved verbatim. This is a story and visual reference,
not an implemented scene or a specification of new game mechanics.

> Darkness, everything is black, the air is cold and heavy, a  
> small piece of the ceiling crumbles and a ray of light is cast on a open tomb inside a dark pit, illuminating a mysterious Knight. The knight slowly wakes up from a deep slumber, reaching his left hand into the tombs edge, pulling him self into sited position. He looks at his arm with a confused look. a stone material is rooted in half of his left chest spreading trough the whole left arm, engraved magical runes spiral from the shoulder into the back of his hand where there is a translucent semi-spherical crystal embedded at the center, half filled with a dark purple liquid, magical runes are engraved in the stone circumscribing the embedded crystal. underneath those engravings there are five small circular slots.

Additional approved choices: faint submerged eye-like shapes; five empty sockets
in a shallow arc toward the wrist; minimal worn burial clothing; three evenly lit
character studies before a cinematic illustration. See the
[approved player brief](../player_concept_brief.md).
