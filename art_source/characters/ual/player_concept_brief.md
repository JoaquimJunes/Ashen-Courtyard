## Approved awakening brief

Neth chose to reinterpret the character while retaining the warrior idea and
broad build. The archetype is a fallen sacred warrior, balancing armor and cloth
in the later equipped design. This cycle instead shows his awakening appearance:
minimal worn burial clothing exposes the stone transformation. Forms are clear
and faceted, with restrained painted textures rather than fixed PS1 limitations.

Reference priority: Neth's script → main mana gauge → protagonist proportions
→ supporting Confessor and Dark Crusader costume references. The source script
is preserved verbatim in [the exploration pack](awakening_concepts/narrative_reference.md).
The approved source for the hand object is the
[main mana gauge](../../ui/soulbound_hud/concepts/evolution-partial-closeup.png).
The HUD's hearts, horizontal health panel and interface layout do not transfer
onto the body. The hand vessel includes faint submerged eye-like shapes.

### Fixed features

- Stone rooted in half of the left chest, spreading over the entire left arm
  and onto the hand, with an irregular embedded boundary against normal flesh.
- Engraved runes spiral from the shoulder down the arm to the hand.
- A translucent hemispherical crystal is embedded centrally in the back of the
  anatomical left hand. Dark-purple liquid fills its lower half; faint eye-like
  shapes remain submerged.
- An engraved rune ring surrounds the crystal. Exactly five small empty circular
  sockets sit below it in a shallow arc toward the wrist, away from the knuckles.
- Violet accents remain restrained. The stone follows human joints and movement.

### Three approved exploration directions

1. Close-fitting stone: broad, quiet surfaces following the body.
2. Fractured stone: shallow layered angular plates with stronger silhouette breaks.
3. Carved stone: worn deliberate surfaces emphasizing spiral inscriptions.

Each sheet pairs a full-body character study with a dorsal left-hand close-up.
Framing and readable neutral lighting support comparison. A dark tomb scene,
full equipped costume and production model are later work.

## Life progression — approved image selection

Permanent maximum-life upgrades strengthen and consolidate the stone without
adding bulk or expanding the chest boundary. Remaining left arm and hand flesh
gradually petrifies, including all fingertips and palm by the final form.
This replaces the earlier fixed-coverage and exposed-fingertip rule.
Current damage and healing do not reverse its
stage. Six forms match the mana gauge's count, but life and mana evolve independently:
Fractured → Binding → Reinforced → Consolidated → Fortified → Unbroken.

Use the revised darker charcoal and varied forked, hooked, intersecting and
diamond-based runes consistently across the sheets. Preserve the spiral route,
spacing and restrained glow. Keep the character, clothing, pose and hand vessel
constant. The crystal stays half full, eyes faint and all five sockets empty
to isolate the change in stone. Exact life thresholds, transition animation and
game integration are deferred. See the
[life-progression pack](awakening_concepts/life_progression/README.md).
